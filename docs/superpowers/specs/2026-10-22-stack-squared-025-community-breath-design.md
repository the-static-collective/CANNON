# STACK² 025 — EVERY REPOSITORY THAT STILL HOLDS BREATH
## Living Commons / Community Breath Field — design candidate

**Status:** experimental, synthetic-only cross-repository study. **Parent:** CANNON experiment `stack-squared-024-living-commons`. **Date:** 2026-10-22. **Human context:** Greater Mankato Coalition of Care gathering, October 24, 2026; Waseca community grocery is an exploratory local use case, **not** a declared partner, adopted service, or verified requirement.

## The large question

What if every still-useful Static Collective project could **make an offer to a real community endeavor** without ceasing to be itself, handing over its authority, or requiring people to join a platform?

This is a **composition of invitations**, not a merger of repositories.

```text
A person names a particular concern or possibility
  -> another human hears it (or says no)
  -> an OFFER / NEED / BUILD / STEWARD card preserves only permissioned public-safe detail
  -> local parties decide whether to relate
  -> a specific act might happen
  -> the affected person may separately confirm it
  -> an optional, attributable return remains reachable
```

**One rule:** a physical card must be fully useful without an account, a camera, an app, a scan, a donation, or a network connection. A digital return door is an enhancement, not the condition for participation.

## CANNON STACK⁸²: eight organs, sixty-four candidate relations

The eight `024` organs are **NOTICE, GATHER, RELATE, OFFER, CREATE, ACT, WITNESS, RETURN**. Their 8×8 matrix contains 64 **questions about possible crossings**, not 64 deployed adapters. The source registry in `labs/living-commons-001/source-registry.json` pins eight experimental project snapshots; pins prove addresses, not adoption, readiness, rights, or consent.

Each source owns its own decision. The CANNON experiment observes, proposes, refuses or holds. It does not announce anyone's deployment as community infrastructure.

## All-repository aperture — not mass activation

`community-breath-census.json` is a point-in-time inventory of accessible repositories with a small README-inspected subset. **NOT_ASSESSED** means exactly that, even if a repository is unarchived. **README_INSPECTED** is a documentary observation, not code verification, branch reconciliation, field proof, endorsement or a promise to include it.

## Two independent currents

**CARE current:** NEED PROPOSED -> STEWARD REVIEW -> OFFER PROPOSED -> HUMAN ACCEPT -> FULFILLMENT REPORTED -> AFFECTED-PARTY CONFIRMED / DISPUTED. A helper's report is **not** delivery confirmation.

**CULTURE current:** SEED -> INDEPENDENT CONTRIBUTION -> INDIVIDUAL CREATOR GRANTS -> EDITORIAL CHOICE -> POSSIBLE RELEASE / WITHDRAWAL. A completed care act confers **zero** music or image rights; a consented song is not proof of community help. Permission to use a participant's story is separate, narrow and revocable before release within the actual publishing process.

**Community return:** a public-safe card or human-introduced connection can link to a future page; no code may presume an existing `abundent.org` endpoint or modify its production deployment. **Human continuation precedes digital scaling.**

## Saturday field shape — three conversations, one retained trace

Offer a light, permission-based table activity, asking organizers before setting anything up. A person may choose **OFFER**, **NEED**, or **BUILD**, or decline. Pair one declared capability with one bounded next action only after both people agree. Record at most a safe public title and an opt-in follow-up channel chosen by the humans themselves. If no real consent exists, leave with no identifying data. Printed prototypes contain deliberately fictional labels.

The sample Waseca grocery question is **Can a willing local producer, community steward, and possible venue voluntarily explore one tiny pilot?** It does not assert a grocery partnership, inventory, supply, pricing, charitable status, or legal organization.

A usable success gate: two willing people explain a particular seed and their next step in under two minutes *with paper alone*. For a subsequent pilot, track one independently confirmed concrete act, not app usage or sign-ups.

## Provenance and guards

1. **No silent across-repo changes**. Each donor keeps its code, release and local authority.
2. **No inferred partner status** from attendance, interest, conversation, website, or public repo.
3. **No personal data** in this branch's synthetic fixtures or published census.
4. **No automatic matching, assignments, money, donations, notices, background collection, AI agents, or publication.**
5. **No fake QR destination.** Printed offline examples state that their continuation is human-led; a real scannable return URL requires a deployed, permissioned, privacy-reviewed endpoint.
6. **No equivalence** between metadata, default branch, open PR, a passing unit test, and a community field trial.
7. **No adoption by branch naming.** This branch is a proposed lab. No merge or CANNON canon selection is requested.
8. **No music publicity from another person's hardship.** Every cultural contribution and subject-story permission is independently chosen.

## Experimental artifacts and acceptance tests

- `labs/living-commons-001/community-breath-census.json`: discovered repository names + inspected documentary pins; unknowns explicitly remain unknown.
- `labs/living-commons-001/breath-audit.mjs`: no-network, fail-closed audit; enumerate known and unknown, reproduce a digest, flag missing metadata, never label unarchived as operational.
- `labs/living-commons-001/field-kit.mjs`: print-ready, **fictional** six-card sheet for the Saturday conversational prototype, no signups or real URLs.
- The existing `024` independent CARE/CULTURE synthetic validator remains the test for the two distinct currents.
- Test malicious personal-data-shaped fields, invented deployments, and implicit permission as hard rejections.

## Gates not crossed

No live integration into another repo, no field test, no Coalition or venue approval, no identity/consent infrastructure, no live payment system, no deployed Abundent portal, and no public service launch. Candidate specimens and optional future plans remain under human review.

**The big smash is not that one machine does everything. It is that everything useful can make an accountable, independent offer—and the community can refuse it.**
