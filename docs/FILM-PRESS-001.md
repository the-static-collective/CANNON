# FILM-PRESS-001 — CANNON cinematic candidate → lemonPRESS held edition

This review-only experiment adds source-native **Haunted Toaster → PlayDeck → Haunted Blender** to the existing **Band Runtime → lemonPRESS PRESS 001** crossing (PR #54). Each of **five separate public owning repositories** executes at an exact pinned Git commit, in its own checkout. CANNON contributes only fixtures, adapters, composition assertions and a cold-replay packet.

1. The **original Toaster sigil grammar** produces and replays SIX deterministic candidate futures from two free, synthetic primitives (no song, live witness or media master). The resulting six *actual source-owner plan hashes* form six logically bound PlayDeck cards.
2. **Original PlayDeck composeDeck** consumes those six cards, fixture track gates, and fixture world rules and emits a validated native composition plan. Media sources are `sigil://...` logical references, not real pixels or downloadable artwork; there is NO Remotion video render in this step.
3. **Original Blender normalize_timing + DREAMBREEDER + Cutout Stage** consumes the Toaster family, the actual PlayDeck composition and synthetic track/lyrics. A **scripted/fictional** KEEP fixture selects one proposal to exercise the real native renderer; this MUST NOT be mistaken for a real person's KEEP. Pillow makes the small background/actor sprites (colors derived from the Toaster plan hash), and the source-owned FFmpeg Cutout renderer emits a **real, silent 96×64 MP4**, 3 seconds, with 4 sections and a non-publishing receipt. Blender proposes an awakening aperture but no generator is called, no accepted take imported. No real track audio or synchronized music video exists.
4. The already-proven CANNON ROOM-VINYL-001 invokes **original Band Runtime** and **original lemonPRESS** to produce the source-preserving PRESS 001 proposal, including a physical HOLD and draft label. A new deterministic **liner-notes JSON/HTML** and **jacket SVG** refer to the actual native Toaster/PlayDeck/Blender outputs. Those extra two printable surfaces are CANNON editorial prototypes **not yet first-class lemonPRESS source-owner renderers**.

Everything is compared byte-for-byte by an independent cold process, including the real silent MP4. A changed liner-note file must fail replay. Original owner suites and source paths are tested in CI, and all five original commits are retained in the receipt.

No song master, music download, human creative choice, actor, actual room message, credit license, artwork consent, distribution, vinyl mold, cutting lathe, actual vinyl record, manufacturing quote, film publication, synced music video, record sale or artist-copy shipment is claimed. The preexisting 12-song/11-creator *Other People's Fire* target remains an editorial concept. Original PRESS 000 still includes only source-linked A1 and an open B-side. DO NOT merge/canonize on behalf of owners.

Run with a local JSON mapping `toaster`, `playdeck`, `blender`, `band`, `lemonpress` to exact pinned local repository checkouts:

```sh
node scripts/film-press-crossing.mjs build --roots roots.json --out /tmp/film-press --authorize SYNTHETIC_TEST_ONLY
node scripts/film-press-crossing.mjs verify --roots roots.json --out /tmp/film-press
```

**Ownership/authority:** generated six-up != musician decision; plan != render; synthetic KEEP != actual human permission; silent movie != licensed song video; printable mockup != press plate; receipt != manufacture/distribution authorization.
